General Meetings

We meet every Monday at 7PM by the practice rooms! That's on the second floor of the Student Center, past Dancing Goats and 91.1 WREK. Come to hear updates for the organization, upcoing local events, and talk with other folks. Members are encouraged to come and non-members are welcome too :)
